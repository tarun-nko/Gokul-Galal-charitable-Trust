Khodan Secondary School Expansion Project

We are excited to announce the expansion of Khodan Secondary School, a major initiative funded by the Gokul Galal Charitable Trust.

Through the generous donations collected from various individuals and organizations, our trust has successfully raised the necessary funds to begin this transformative project. The expansion will provide better educational facilities and opportunities for students in the region.

Status: Fundraising completed successfully. Construction work will begin soon.
