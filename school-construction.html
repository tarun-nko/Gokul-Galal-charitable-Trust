Mahatma Gandhi Government School (Khodan) — New Campus Construction Nearing Completion

Construction Ongoing — Almost Complete Finishing Phase (~92%)

Mahatma Gandhi Government School, Khodan

Building a Modern, Inspiring Educational Haven for Rural Youth

We are thrilled to share that the construction of the brand-new school building for Mahatma Gandhi Government School at Khodan is now in its final stages and nearing completion! Spearheaded by the Gokul Galal Charitable Trust, this transformative initiative upgrades educational infrastructure for generations of students.

With enthusiastic mobilization of community funds and generous contributions, the multi-story academic campus has fully taken shape. The structural framing, brick masonry, arched colonnade walkways, and roof slabs are 100% complete. Active work is now focused on final finishing touches including decorative window grills, exterior and interior paint, safety staircase railings, electrical fixtures, and courtyard landscaping.
