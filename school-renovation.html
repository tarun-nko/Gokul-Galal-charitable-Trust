Khodan Secondary School Renovation & Expansion

Renovation and expansion work is actively in progress at Khodan Secondary School, a transformative initiative spearheaded and supported by the Gokul Galal Charitable Trust.

Following the successful mobilization of community funds and generous contributions, structural development is actively underway. The renovation project features new modern classrooms, arched corridors, enhanced natural ventilation, upgraded learning facilities, and safe campus infrastructure designed to empower students across the region.

Status: Renovation Work In Progress Construction, architectural remodeling, and campus upgrade work are actively underway.
